What we do

We carry the risk, end to end

Outsider Art Events is the contracting company behind every event we run. One accountable team, from the first venue conversation to the final load-out.

Venue and production

Venue contracts, floor plans, build, power, security and health and safety, delivered to the standard large venues expect.

  • Venue contracting
  • Site plans and build
  • Event safety management

Artists and exhibitors

Curated selection, clear agreements and a well-run show floor, so the people the event is built around can do their best work.

  • Applications and curation
  • Artist agreements
  • On-site artist support

Ticketing and audience

Ticketing, marketing and visitor experience, from the first announcement to the doors opening and the crowd going home.

  • Ticketing and access
  • Campaigns and media
  • Visitor services

Partnerships

Sponsorship and brand partnerships built around the audience, with clear packages and straight reporting afterwards.

  • Sponsorship packages
  • Brand activations
  • Post-event reporting
Our approach

From the outside, in

Structure first.

Contracts, insurance and logistics are settled early and properly. That is what lets the creative side take risks.

Respect for the craft.

Every event is built around the artists and the community who make it, with their input from the start.

Straight answers.

Venues, sponsors and artists always know what is agreed, what it costs and when it happens.
